During a gathering

  1. Start in Sermon mode. Everyone listens while the pastor leads.
  2. Watch the Hands list. Hands are kept in the order they were raised. Tap Invite to give someone the mic, then Done when they finish.
  3. Open the floor only for times of open prayer, then return to Sermon mode.
  4. Tap a person to mute, remove, or (overflow room) press and hold to pray with them privately.
  5. Report problems such as scammers or anyone pretending to be the Apostle or a pastor. Remove them first, then tell the admins.
